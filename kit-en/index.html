    About this copy

    Nexora Quote Desk v2.0 — single-file offline HTML. No accounts, no cloud, no network requests.

    Licence. Use inside one business, including its employees and work devices. Resale, redistribution or passing the file on is not allowed. Estimates and PDFs you produce may be sent to your customers.

    Calculation and tax disclaimer. This is a quoting helper. Check your inputs, the tax rate you apply and the result before you send an estimate or sign a contract. Nothing here is tax, legal or accounting advice.

    Signature. The signature pad captures a handwritten mark for the customer's confirmation. The checksum printed with it detects later edits; it is not identity verification, tamper-proof sealing or a legally binding e-signature.

    Data. Everything stays in this browser's storage. Clearing site data, private windows or a broken machine can lose it — keep JSON backups.
