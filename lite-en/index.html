First time? 3-minute walkthrough
  1. Type your business details once. They stay in this browser for next time.
  2. Fill in the customer and job; estimate number, date and valid-until fill themselves in.
  3. Add line items with quantity, unit and your cost per unit.
  4. Set your target margin on the right — each unit price is calculated for you.
  5. Mark lines Tax where your state charges sales tax (most states tax materials, many do not tax labor).
  6. Check the customer view below, then Print / Save PDF.
